Research question

Can we edit when a sound happens?

Audio editing systems commonly add, remove, or replace sounds. TempoEdit studies a complementary problem: preserving a sound's identity while changing its temporal behavior inside a complex mixture.

The project investigates signals already present inside a pretrained text-to-audio model. By guiding its internal attention and generative trajectory, we aim to achieve flexible temporal edits without retraining the model for every operation.

01

Relocate

Move a selected sound event to a different moment in the scene.

Extend

Lengthen the event while preserving its acoustic character.

Shorten

Compress its active interval without disturbing the surrounding audio.

Proposed direction

One frozen model.
Multiple temporal edits.

Rather than training a separate editor for each operation, the project treats temporal editing as inference-time guidance over a pretrained text-to-audio flow model.

  1. 01
    Observe

    Locate the target event within a polyphonic mixture.

  2. 02
    Guide

    Use the model's internal signals to define the desired timing.

  3. 03
    Optimize

    Adapt the generative trajectory at inference time.

  4. 04
    Compose

    Produce the edited scene while preserving non-target content.
